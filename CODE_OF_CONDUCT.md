# Code of Conduct

Fulbr is a technical project with a security and privacy focus. We want participation to
be safe, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems, not hostile to people.
- Assume good faith, but require evidence for security claims.
- Respect different experience levels and languages; English and Russian are both welcome.
- Keep discussions on topic and avoid sharing personal data.
- Do not post credentials, private keys, tokens, or real subscription URLs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.
- Deliberately submitting malware, hidden telemetry, or fake benchmarks.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken security or bypass consent requirements.
- Using the project to attack or surveil others.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants. Reports should be sent privately to the
maintainers once a contact channel is published. Do not open a public issue with sensitive
details.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, discussions, and any project-run
community space.
